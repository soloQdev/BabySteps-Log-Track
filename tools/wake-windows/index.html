About these ranges

The ranges come from Cleveland Clinic's guide to wake windows by age, and they are the same ranges used in the BabySteps app. They are typical ranges, not targets.

Signs such as rubbing eyes, yawning, looking away or getting fussy often show tiredness before the clock does. If you have questions about your baby's sleep, a health professional can help.

Source: Cleveland Clinic, “Wake windows by age”.

General information, not medical advice

This page gives general information and is not medical advice. If you have any questions or concerns about your baby's growth, feeding or sleep, talk to your doctor, nurse or another health professional.
